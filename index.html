One step at a time.

Use these four moves whenever you get stuck.

  1. 1 · DefineWhat’s the problem?

    Compare your goal with what happens.

  2. 2 · PrepareWhat could I change?

    Look at the code. Pick one small idea.

  3. 3 · TryWhat happens if…?

    Test your change in Web Lab.

  4. 4 · ReflectWhat did I learn?

    Compare the result. Explain the idea.

It’s a loop. Still stuck? Go back, choose another idea, and try again.

You can ask your teacher for help at any point.

About this practice tool

This offline prototype uses scripted questions. Choose your lesson so the Coach can use concepts you have reached. These lesson pathways respond to a limited set of typed answers and clear choices. They do not cover every Web Lab problem. It doesn’t run your code or save the session. Reloading starts over.
